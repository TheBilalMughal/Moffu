# Legal, Consent and Analytics

## Legal
Public Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Privacy Requests; future subprocessors/AUP/DPA when real. Legal documents are jurisdiction/locale/version/status aware and published versions immutable. Signup distinguishes Terms acceptance from Privacy acknowledgment. Material-change/reacceptance behavior is configured, not guessed.

## Consent
Categories STRICTLY_NECESSARY, FUNCTIONAL, ANALYTICS, MARKETING only when configured. Optional tracking fails closed if consent unknown. Clear accept/reject/manage where applicable; no dark patterns or fake cookie banner. GPC/privacy signals route through jurisdiction-aware engine.

## Analytics domains
1) Customer reputation analytics (authoritative internal reporting layer), 2) product analytics, 3) revenue/business analytics, 4) operational observability. Never conflate sources.

PostHog is V1 product analytics provider behind abstraction, not canonical state. No raw review/prompt/support/Brand Brain/payment secrets in external analytics. Stable event schemas/keys; backend-confirmed events for authoritative outcomes. Session replay/heatmaps OFF V1.

Metrics are centrally versioned. Response time is provider review timestamp -> confirmed published reply; AI generation is separate. Reports show freshness/coverage and obey scope/privacy/provider policies.
